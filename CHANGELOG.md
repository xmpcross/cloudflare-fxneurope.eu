# Changelog

All notable changes to Synth are documented here.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-21

- Updated Astro to 7.4.1 and other dependencies.
- Updated `AGENTS.md` for Astro 7.
- Files check out with LF line endings on every platform.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-04

- Initial commercial release.
- Astro 7 theme for AI products with a homepage, pricing page, about page, careers page, industry page, contact page, blog index, topic archives, post, search, RSS, robots, sitemap, and 404 routes.
- Canvas dither engine drawing slow gradient fields with a fine ordered grain, pausing off screen and still under reduced motion.
- Solid header that hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up, with a full-width mega menu, hover intent, keyboard support, and a full-screen mobile menu.
- Homepage sections: a photo hero with a live chat widget, a wordmark logo wall, count-up stats, a three-tab product tour, an answer trace, a model routing table, photographic industry panels, a swipeable customer-story carousel with portraits, security, compliance standards, FAQ, latest posts, and a closing band.
- Homepage copy, navigation, mega menu, and FAQ in config files.
- Pricing page with plan cards, a billing toggle, a grouped comparison table with a sticky plan header and a one-plan view on phones, and its own FAQ.
- Markdown and MDX blog with a validated schema, credited covers, topics, authors, a lead post, pagination, a table of contents with scroll-spy, a reading-progress bar, copyable code blocks, co-authors with portraits, and a call-to-action band placed from frontmatter.
- About page with the company story, milestones, greyscale leadership portraits, and a spinning team globe.
- Careers page with values, benefits, and open roles grouped and filterable by team, configured in one file.
- Industry page template, shipped as Retail, with an intent table, an interactive peak-season chart, and connected systems.
- Contact page with a topic picker, team inboxes, and office addresses, linked from the demo button, pricing plans, and footer.
- Share sheet with eleven networks and a copyable page link on every post.
- Client-side blog search with a keyboard palette and a results page reading one build-time index.
- Canonical URLs, sitemap, RSS, Open Graph, Twitter/X cards, and JSON-LD for the site and every post.
- Self-hosted Geist and Geist Mono, Lucide icons, one orange accent, and no border radius.
- Cover script that renders a white line icon on a dithered field as each post's cover and share image.
- Ten demo posts across five topics with generated covers.
